## About Me

I am **Dr. Khouloud Abid**, a Postdoctoral Researcher at **CNR-ITAE in Messina, Italy**.

I obtained double doctoral degrees in **Engineering in Chemistry and Materials Construction** from the University of Messina, Italy, and in **Physics** from the University of Sfax, Tunisia.

My research career combines **advanced materials, nanotechnology, electrochemistry, plasmonic sensing, and sustainable energy technologies**. During my PhD and two years of postdoctoral research at CNR-IPCF and the University of Messina, I focused on the synthesis and characterization of 2D materials and their integration into electrochemical and plasmonic sensors for sensitive detection of target analytes.

Since 2025, my postdoctoral research at CNR-ITAE has broadened my research interests toward **energy storage and CO₂ capture**, with particular interest in porous carbon materials and thermochemical energy storage.

## Research Approach

My research integrates **material synthesis, structural and physicochemical characterization, electrochemical measurements, and performance evaluation** to establish relationships between material properties and device functionality.

I aim to develop **innovative, scalable and sustainable technologies** addressing challenges in clean energy, CO₂ management, environmental monitoring and next-generation sensing systems.


My expertise includes synthesis, functionalization,
and advanced characterization of nanomaterials.
